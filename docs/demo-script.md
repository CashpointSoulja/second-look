# Demo script: 2 minutes

**Before you go on stage:** open the live URL on `/disputes` and tap **Reset demo data**, so every order is back to DELIVERED. Keep the phone or laptop on `/orders`.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | `/orders` | "Fleek sells graded bundles. When a bundle arrives below grade, the buyer has to prove it themselves. One reseller put it as: 'ordered A/B grade, most were grade C.' Second Look is the check at the moment the parcel is opened." |
| 0:15 | Open **Lululemon activewear bundle** | "Three pieces, sold as A/B, £58. Each one shows the supplier's listing photo and grade." |
| 0:25 | Piece 1, **Upload received photo** (or *use the sample arrival photo*) | "I photograph what arrived…" *(scan sweeps)* "…and it compares the photo with the listing and the flaws the supplier disclosed." |
| 0:40 | Red **BELOW GRADE** badge, box on the thigh | "Listed A, arrived C. There's a bleach stain on the thigh that isn't in the listing. It gives a reason, a confidence score, and where it looked." |
| 0:55 | Piece 2, sample photo → green **MATCH**, blue box | "The jacket has cuff pilling, but the supplier disclosed it, so it's blue and doesn't count. Suppliers only pay for damage that's new or worse." |
| 1:05 | Piece 3, sample photo → red, hole + seam | "Hole at the knee, pulled seam: a new defect." |
| 1:15 | **Build dispute pack** | "One tap." |
| 1:20 | Dispute pack: refund maths | "Each flagged piece shows its maths. The £18 legging drops from A to C, and C is worth 0.4 of A, so the refund is £10.80. Total £20.40, which is 35% of the order." |
| 1:35 | Message, then **Copy message** | "A firm, factual message with photo links and a 48-hour reply request. The buyer reviews it and sends it. Nothing is filed or paid automatically." |
| 1:45 | `/disputes` ledger | "Every delivery, what was flagged and what it's worth." |
| 1:50 | `/pitch` roadmap slide | "Next, we blind-test against QC decisions and pilot with 20 buyers, measuring minutes to an evidence-ready pack. Later, the same scan runs at dispatch, so grades are trusted at both ends." |

**If the AI is slow or down:** the app falls back to scripted verdicts and labels them "Scripted demo verdict". Say so openly. The loop is identical.

**Expected numbers (seeded data, with the scripted or matching AI verdicts):**
- Legging: £18 × (1 − 0.4/1.0) = **£10.80**
- Wunder Under: £16 × 0.6 = **£9.60**
- Order FLK-24817 total: **£20.40**
